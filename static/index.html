Indian Road Safety Protocols & Defensive Guidelines

Key protocols based on Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) standards.

🌧️ Monsoon Hydroplaning & Wet Tarmac

  • Tyre Tread Depth: Ensure minimum 3.0 mm tread depth. Worn tyres lose steering contact over standing water at speeds > 65 km/h.
  • Do Not Brake Harshly: If your vehicle begins to slide, ease off the accelerator smoothly; do not slam the brake pedal.
  • Avoid Flooded Underpasses: In major metros, underpasses can flood rapidly during cloudbursts causing engine hydraulic lock.

🌫️ North India Dense Winter Fog Protocols

  • Never Use High Beams: High-beam rays reflect off airborne moisture particles, blinding the driver. Use low-beams and dedicated amber fog lights.
  • Strictly No Shoulder Parking: Disoriented vehicles following road borders will ram into stationary vehicles parked on highway shoulders.
  • Follow Lane Markers: Track the reflective white painted lines and cats-eye studs on the left side of your lane.

🏍️ Two-Wheeler Defensive Riding

  • ISI Full-Face Helmet: Wear a genuine ISI / DOT certified full-face helmet with strapped buckle.
  • Heavy Truck Blind Spots: Stay out of the blind zones directly in front of truck cabins and beside passenger doors.
  • High-Visibility Neon: Wear reflective straps or fluorescent jackets when commuting at dusk or dawn.

🛣️ Modern Expressway Lane Etiquette

  • Right Lane is Only for Overtaking: Cruising in the fast right lane forces dangerous left-side passing maneuvers. Return to center lane once clear.
  • No Reversing on Exits: If you miss an expressway toll exit, proceed safely to the next interchange. Never reverse.
  • Mandatory Seatbelts All Seats: Unbuckled rear passengers in 100 km/h impacts frequently cause fatal trauma to front passengers.
